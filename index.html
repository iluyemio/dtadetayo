＋ The working model

How I close that gap permanently

  1. 01

    Research first

    Before any design conversation, I dig into your business: your market, your competitors, your customers, and where the current brand is helping or holding you back. Design without this step is just decoration.

  2. 02

    Strategy, then direction

    Research becomes positioning and messaging: what your brand actually stands for, and the reason customers pick you in a crowded market. Every visual decision gets measured against this foundation — grounded in evidence, not personal taste or passing trends.

  3. 03

    A visual system, not a one-off

    Strategy becomes a visual identity: logo, colour, type, and a design language your team can actually use, not just admire. Built to extend across new products, new markets, and new team members without losing coherence or needing a rebuild.

  4. 04

    Equip the team

    A brand only lasts if the people running it can carry it without me. I train founders and teams to execute inside the system independently, so quality doesn't bottleneck at one desk.
